Format, test plan, eligibility and an 8-week study plan for AACN's progressive care exam

Items150 (125 scored, 25 unscored pretest)

Exam tip The pass mark is a raw count, not a percentage, and can change. AACN does not say which items are unscored, so treat each as if it counts.

Exam tip Endocrine through renal add up to 27%, more than cardiovascular. Testable nursing actions include QTc and ST monitoring, sheaths, chest tubes, BiPAP, insulin infusions, NIHSS, CAM and CIWA.

Who can take it

  • Current, unencumbered U.S. RN or APRN license
  • Two-year option: 1,750 hours caring for acutely ill adults, 875 in the most recent year
  • Five-year option: 2,000 hours, 144 in the most recent year
  • Hours in a U.S.- or Canada-based facility, or one with Magnet or JCI status
  • Orientation counts only when you hold the patient assignment
  • Knowledge Professional pathway (educators, managers): 1,040 hours in 2 years, 260 in the most recent year; same exam

Acuity, not unit name, decides: stepdown, telemetry, intermediate care and ED nurses may qualify. Testing window after approval: normally 90 days, currently 180.

How to study: 8 weeks

  1. Day 1: Set your exam date in Settings and take a baseline Mock Exam. Then clear Daily Review daily, plus a weekly Professional Caring set.
  2. Weeks 1 to 2: Cardiovascular. One-Pagers: Rhythm Gallery, ACLS Algorithms in Brief, Hemodynamic Normals, Shock Types at a Glance.
  3. Week 3: Respiratory. One-Pagers: ABG Interpretation in 6 Steps, Capnography, Ventilator Modes and Settings.
  4. Weeks 4 to 5: Endocrine, Heme, Neuro, GI, Renal. One-Pager: Electrolyte Imbalances.
  5. Week 6: Musculoskeletal, Multisystem, Behavioral. One-Pagers: Sepsis and Septic Shock Bundle; Vasopressors and Inotropes; Sedation, Pain and Delirium Scales.
  6. Week 7: Second Mock Exam, then Weakest Subject and Missed Questions.
  7. Week 8: Last Mock Exam early, then only Daily Review and Trouble Questions.
Exam tip Most items test application and analysis, not just recall. AACN calls PCCN patients moderately stable but at elevated risk of instability: expect items on what to notice or do first.

Test-day tips

  • Current government photo ID with signature, matching your AACN record; no military or temporary IDs
  • Testing center: more than 15 minutes late means no admission; scratch paper on request
  • Remote: run PSI's Tutorial Test on the same computer and Wi-Fi; check in 15 to 30 minutes early (it can take 60+ minutes); on-screen notepad, no scratch paper
  • Bookmark hard items and return; answer every item, as guessing is not penalized
  • Up to two 5-minute breaks; the clock keeps running
  • Leaving the testing area without permission voids the exam; phones, notes or copying items mean dismissal, no refund
