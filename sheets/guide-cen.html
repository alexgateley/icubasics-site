Exam tip You score one point per right answer and lose nothing for a wrong guess, so answer every item. BCEN does not say which 25 items are pretest, so treat each one as scored. Allow about 1 minute per item.

Exam tip Cardiovascular, respiratory and neurological add up to 52 items, about a third of your score.
Exam tip There is no trauma domain. Trauma is tested inside each system (chest, head and spine, abdomen, GU/OB, face and eye) and in Musculoskeletal and Wound.
  • Shock: cardiogenic and obstructive (Cardiovascular), hypovolemic and distributive, plus sepsis (Medical), neurogenic (Neurological)
  • Overdose: intentional (Mental Health), substance use and withdrawal (Medical), CO, cyanide, acids and alkalis (Toxicology)
  • Pediatrics has no domain: croup, intussusception and neonatal resuscitation sit in the systems
  • Easy to miss: forensic evidence, drug diversion, workplace violence, cultural humility

Changed from the 2022 outline:

  • Gastrointestinal is its own domain; GI plus GU/GYN/OB rose from 18 to 24 items
  • HEENT replaces Maxillofacial and Ocular
  • Professional Issues: Prioritization (triage, mass casualty, throughput) plus Legal and ethical
  • No longer listed: envenomation, pain management, procedural sedation

Who can take it

  • Current, unencumbered RN license in the US, a US territory, Canada or Australia
  • RN-equivalent license from another country: accepted once verified through TruMerit
  • Two years of emergency experience recommended, not required
  • No current clinical job needed
  • Any active restriction, suspension or probation disqualifies you
  • English only, in every country

How to study: an 8-week plan

  1. Week 1: read BCEN's outline and 10 sample questions (they show format, not difficulty), then take a full Mock Exam as a baseline.
  2. Weeks 1-2: Cardiovascular, Respiratory, Neurological; Rhythm Gallery and ACLS Algorithms in Brief One-Pagers.
  3. Weeks 3-4: Medical, GI, GU/GYN/OB, Mental Health; Sepsis Bundle, Shock Types and Electrolyte One-Pagers.
  4. Weeks 5-6: Musculoskeletal and Wound, HEENT, Environment/Toxicology, Professional Issues.
  5. Every day: clear Daily Review first; spaced repetition brings back questions you missed or were unsure of.
  6. Every week: one Weakest Subject session and a pass through Trouble Questions.
  7. Weeks 7-8: two timed Mock Exams (BCEN's practice exam is optional), read every rationale, rest the last 2 days.

Test-day tips

  • Arrive (test center) or log in (remote) 30 minutes early; expect a photo, and wanding at test centers
  • Remote: personal computer only (employer laptops often fail); run PSI's system check and tutorial 3+ days ahead
  • ID: unexpired government photo ID with signature, such as a passport, driver's license or state ID (not a military ID)
  • Testing outside your home country: bring your passport
  • Your first and last name must match your registration exactly
  • Up to 2 breaks of 10 minutes or less; the clock keeps running
  • Scratch paper: 2 sheets (given at test centers, your own blank sheets remotely), destroyed at the end
Safety A break over 10 minutes ends your exam. A no-show, late arrival or wrong ID can cost the full fee. To keep your fee, reschedule more than 24 hours ahead and inside your window.
