If you test before 2027

  • TMC: last applications December 15, 2026; last test day December 31, 2026.
  • Passed the TMC at the high cut before December 31, 2026? You may take the CSE through December 31, 2027, or the RT exam if you meet its admission rules.
  • Current CRTs who meet RT admission rules can test for the RRT; scoring below the low cut keeps the CRT.
  • TMC and CSE attempts do not count toward RT exam attempts.
  • Credential maintenance is unchanged.

CRT-to-Registry admission routes end December 31, 2026. From 2027, a CRT without the RT degree or CSRT RRT cannot enter the RT exam.

Chronic lung disease: invasive ventilation 5, noninvasive 6, outpatient 3, new diagnosis 3. Judgment type: 20 information gathering, 40 decision making. Neonatal or pediatric items across the whole exam: 15 to 18.

Exam tip Only 23 of 160 scored items test recall, and every clinical judgment item is application or analysis. Practice choosing the next step, not reciting facts.
Exam tip The largest single task is device troubleshooting: 19 of the 25 Devices items. Next come oxygenation and ventilation support (10) and care-plan changes (10).

How to study: 8 weeks

  1. Week 1: read the content outline, then take NBRC's free RT practice exam and a timed app Mock Exam as baselines.
  2. Weeks 1 to 2: Patient Data, with the ABG Interpretation and Capnography One-Pagers.
  3. Weeks 3 to 4: Interventions, with the Ventilator Modes and Settings One-Pager. Troubleshoot each device as you go (ventilators, CPAP/NPPV, high-flow, resuscitation, airways).
  4. Week 5: Devices and Patient Safety: troubleshooting, then infection control and quality control.
  5. Week 6: both Clinical Judgment subjects. For each stem, ask what to assess or what to change.
  6. Daily: Daily Review; twice a week, a Weakest Subject set.
  7. Weeks 7 to 8: two more Mock Exams, Missed and Trouble Questions, and an official NBRC Self-Assessment Exam if you choose.
Exam tip The real exam allows about 78 seconds per item. At that pace, the 160-item app Mock Exam takes about 3.5 hours.

Test-day tips

  • Bring two current IDs with your name and signature, one with a photo. No temporary IDs.
  • Over 15 minutes late: no test, no refund.
  • No personal calculator; use the on-screen one. Scratch paper comes one sheet at a time.
  • Breaks are allowed, but the clock keeps running.
  • Bookmark hard items and come back. Answer every item; there is no guessing penalty.
  • Pretest items look like scored items, so give each one full effort.
  • Turn your phone off and leave it in the locker. If it rings or vibrates, your test ends.
