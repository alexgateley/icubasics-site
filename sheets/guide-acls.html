Exam tip The exam reflects 2025 science. Know these changes:
  • One 6-link Chain of Survival; SVT renamed narrow-complex tachycardia
  • Sotalol dropped from the tachycardia algorithm
  • Synchronized starting doses: 100 J narrow-complex or monomorphic VT; 200 J AF or flutter
  • IV first, then IO; no drugs via ET tube; squeeze the bag one-third to one-half for chest rise
  • Post-ROSC: MAP at least 65 mm Hg, SpO₂ 90 to 98%
  • Not following commands after ROSC: 32 to 37.5 °C for at least 36 h
  • Tenecteplase for stroke; LBBB alone no longer defines STEMI
Safety Values are typical adult figures for exam study. Follow your institution's or agency's protocols and device guidance.

Who can take it

  • Healthcare professionals who lead or join cardiac arrest, stroke and cardiopulmonary emergency care
  • Expected skills: high-quality BLS, ECG reading, ACLS drugs, bag-mask ventilation
  • BLS card: not required by AHA, though your Training Center may; some classes add one
  • Classroom: Precourse Self-Assessment at 70% or higher; print and bring the report
  • HeartCode: bring your online completion certificate to the skills session
  • Update (renewal) course: shorter; ask your Training Center if you qualify
Exam tip The Precourse Self-Assessment has 3 sections: rhythm recognition, pharmacology and practical application (20 items each on the 2025 written form).

How to study (8 weeks)

  1. Week 1: free AHA Precourse Self-Assessment and one app Mock Exam as baselines; start Daily Review.
  2. Weeks 1 to 2: Rhythm Recognition with the Rhythm Gallery One-Pager.
  3. Weeks 3 to 4: ACLS Algorithms and Pharmacology with the ACLS Algorithms in Brief One-Pager; say each next step aloud.
  4. Week 5: BLS, Post-Cardiac Arrest Care, Special Situations and the Capnography One-Pager.
  5. Week 6: Weakest Subject and Level Up sessions, then Missed Questions.
  6. Week 7: rehearse leading Megacode cases aloud with the testing checklists in your manual's appendix; finish any prework.
  7. Week 8: two full Mock Exams (aim well above 84%) and Trouble Questions.
Exam tip Megacode cases open with a bradycardia or tachycardia, pass through a shockable and a nonshockable arrest rhythm, and end with post-arrest care.

Test-day tips

  • Bring your passing self-assessment report or HeartCode certificate
  • Bring the provider manual (print or eBook) with algorithms and drug tables tabbed
  • Open resource is not open time: answer what you know, then look up flagged items
  • Megacode leader: assign roles, use closed-loop talk, keep CCF above 80%, check rhythm every 2 minutes
  • Not passed? Your Training Center sets remediation and retesting
