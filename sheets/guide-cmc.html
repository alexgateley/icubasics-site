New or expanded from 11/19/2026:

  • Cardiac arrest; SCAD under ACS
  • VADs, temporary and durable (such as Impella, LVAD)
  • Drugs: ACEI, ARB, ARNI, MRA, SGLT2i, beta-blockers, CCBs, lipid-lowering, pulmonary vasodilators, sedation and analgesia
  • Delirium; fluid and acid-base imbalances
  • Chest tubes, oxygen devices, cardiac rehab, cardiac MRI and CT
  • Adrenal disorders dropped
Exam tip Therapeutic interventions are now 44%: drugs, pacing, ICD and CRT, cath lab care, circulatory support and renal replacement (CRRT, SCUF, hemodialysis).

Who can take it

  • Current, unencumbered U.S. RN or APRN license
  • ABSNC- or NCCA-accredited direct-care adult specialty certification, such as CCRN (direct or tele-critical care pathway), PCCN (direct care pathway) or CEN; shown as CCRN-CMC
  • 1,750 direct-care hours with acutely or critically ill adults in 2 years (875 in the last year, 875 cardiac), or 2,000 in 5 years (144 in the last year, 1,000 cardiac)
  • Hours in the U.S. or Canada, or abroad at a Magnet or JCI-accredited facility
  • Audit verifier: clinical supervisor or RN or physician colleague; not yourself or a relative

CMC goes Inactive if the tied certification lapses. Non-AACN proof must be valid 90 days past applying.

Renew every 3 years: 432 hours with acutely or critically ill adult cardiac patients (144 in the last year), a current specialty certification, and 25 Category A Clinical Judgment cardiology CERPs or a passed exam.

How to study

  1. Week 1: Take a Mock Exam cold for a baseline; read the new test plan. Start Daily Review.
  2. Weeks 2 to 3: Therapeutic interventions, with the IABP Timing, Vasopressors and Inotropes, and Ventilator Modes and Settings One-Pagers. Add Impella, ECMO and CRRT.
  3. Week 4: Cardiovascular conditions, with Rhythm Gallery and ACLS Algorithms in Brief.
  4. Week 5: Non-cardiovascular conditions, with Shock Types at a Glance, Electrolyte Imbalances, ABG Interpretation in 6 Steps and Sedation, Pain and Delirium Scales.
  5. Week 6: Monitoring and diagnostics, with Hemodynamic Normals and Capnography. Second Mock Exam.
  6. Week 7: Weakest Subject daily; rework Trouble Questions. Third Mock Exam.
  7. Week 8: Daily Review, flagged questions, One-Pager skim. Last Mock Exam 3 or more days out.

Test-day tips

  • Current government photo ID with signature, matching your AACN record. No military or temporary IDs.
  • Test center: arrive by your appointment time; over 15 minutes late, no admission. Scratch paper on request.
  • Remote: check in 15 to 30 minutes early; check-in can take 60+ minutes. Over 30 minutes late, no admission. Digital notepad only.
  • Pace about 80 seconds per item; bookmark hard ones and go back.
  • No guessing penalty: answer every item.
  • Up to two 5-minute breaks; the clock keeps running.

Exam content is confidential; sharing it can lead to revocation of certification.

Exam tip AACN is moving to generic drug names only. Learn drugs by generic name.
