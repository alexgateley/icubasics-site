Exam tip Endo through Integumentary (21%) is the largest area; Cardiovascular fell from 17% to 13%. Testable actions include circulatory support devices, temporary pacing, CRRT, ECMO, proning, targeted temperature management and organ donation. Professional Caring tests seven Synergy Model competencies, such as advocacy.

Who can take it

  • Current, unencumbered U.S. RN or APRN license
  • Two-year option: 1,750 hours with acutely/critically ill adults in 2 years, 875 in the most recent year
  • Five-year option: 2,000 hours over 5 years, 144 in the most recent year
  • Most hours, overall and in the year before applying, must be with critically ill patients
  • Facility in the U.S. or Canada, or elsewhere with Magnet or JCI status
  • Orientation counts only when you hold the assignment
  • Educators, managers, APRNs and preceptors may count bedside supervision if directly involved in care

Acuity, not unit name, decides: ICU, ED, trauma, flight and nurse anesthesia hours can count. Other pathways: Tele-critical Care, Knowledge Professional. Testing window: currently 180 days (normally 90).

How to study: 8 weeks

  1. Week 1: baseline Mock Exam, then Cardiovascular. One-Pagers: Rhythm Gallery, ACLS Algorithms, Hemodynamic Normals, IABP Timing, Vasopressors and Inotropes.
  2. Week 2: Respiratory. One-Pagers: Ventilator Modes, Capnography.
  3. Weeks 3 to 4: Endo, Heme, GI, Renal and Integumentary.
  4. Week 5: Musculoskeletal, Neuro and Behavioral. One-Pager: Sedation Scales.
  5. Week 6: Multisystem. One-Pagers: Sepsis Bundle, Shock Types, ABG Interpretation, Electrolyte Imbalances.
  6. Week 7: Professional Caring practice, a second Mock Exam, then Weakest Subject and Missed Questions.
  7. Week 8: final Mock Exam early, then Daily Review and Trouble Questions only. Rest the day before.
Exam tip Clear Daily Review every day. AACN suggests weighting study time by your baseline area scores; most new certificants started about three months out.

Test-day tips

  • Current government photo ID with signature, name matching your AACN record; no military or temporary ID
  • Schedule 24+ hours ahead; reschedule or cancel up to 48 hours before
  • Testing center: over 15 minutes late means no admission
  • Remote: check in 15 to 30 minutes early (no entry if over 30 minutes late); check-in can take an hour; run PSI's Tutorial Test; digital notepad only
  • Learn generic drug names; AACN is moving to generic-only items
  • Answer every item; guessing is not penalized
  • Up to two 5-minute breaks; the clock keeps running
Safety Leaving the testing area without permission voids the exam. Phones, notes or recording can mean dismissal: no score, no refund. Remote proctors can warn or end the exam for looking away, covering your mouth or talking.
